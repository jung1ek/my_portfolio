## What I wanted on screen

- A point on the unit circle
- The same point as a real/imaginary pair
- A slider for θ

![Placeholder figure](/images/blog/euler.svg)

The animation is almost embarrassing in its simplicity. Rotation is multiplication. Once you see the point travel, the identity stops being a poster on a classroom wall.

> I did not understand Euler's formula until I could drag the angle and watch the cosine and sine tracks update together.

## What I would change

The first version used too much UI chrome. The second version is a canvas, a slider, and a line of latex. That is the whole product.
