I do not write because I have conclusions. I write because a half-formed idea looks different in a sentence than it does in a comment.

A public notebook has a useful cost. You cannot hide behind “I will clean this up later.” The page is the cleanup.

If this site ever feels too polished, that is a smell. The drafts should stay a little uneven.
