## Problem

Most of my early NLP sketches had no baseline and no evaluation. They were demos with feelings.

## Solution

A notebook-shaped project with three locked pieces: a dataset card, a baseline, and a table of deltas.

## Features

- Reproducible tokenization experiments
- A majority-class / bag-of-words baseline
- A one-page report template

## Development process

I banned new model ideas until the baseline was written down. That rule deleted a surprising amount of work.

## Challenges

Data leakage through preprocessing. Once I logged the exact split, the “clever” model looked ordinary.
